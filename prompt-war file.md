# **3. Customer Signals**

A survey of 2,000 active and recently inactive customers found:

· 38% — Prices/fees feel higher than expected

· 34% — Delivery takes too long

· 29% — Products shown as available sometimes become unavailable after ordering

· 24% — Discounts are confusing

· 21% — Prefer purchasing directly from nearby stores

· 18% — Difficult to discover relevant local products

· 16% — Experienced refund problems

· 14% — App experience feels cluttered

· 11% — Delivery tracking is inaccurate

**Important signal:** 61% of customers who stopped ordering had previously rated NOVA CART 4★ or higher.

# **4. Customer Behaviour**

· 54% of new users complete their first order.

· Only 31% place a second order within 30 days.

· Customers completing 3 orders have a 72% probability of ordering again the following month.

· Customers acquired through large first-order discounts show lower long-term retention than organically acquired users.

· 44% of promotional coupons are never redeemed.

· 19% of customers repeatedly search for products unavailable from nearby stores.

· Customers purchasing across multiple store categories show higher repeat usage.

# **5. Operations**

Recent order data shows:

· 11% — Cancelled

· 13% — Delivered more than 15 minutes after the estimated time

· 8% — Contained at least one substituted item

· 6% — Required a refund/support interaction

## **Cancellation Reasons**

· 35% — Product unavailable

· 27% — Customer cancelled because of delay

· 18% — Store rejected the order

· 12% — Delivery partner unavailable

· 8% — Other reasons

Many partner stores manually maintain inventory. Some update stock multiple times per day, while others update it only once every 1–3 days.

# **6. Partner Store Signals**

NOVA CART interviewed 100 partner stores:

· 46% believe NOVA CART brings valuable additional customers.

· 39% say maintaining online inventory requires too much effort.

· 31% believe promotions reduce their margins.

· 28% struggle to predict which products will sell online.

· 23% occasionally reject orders during busy periods.

· 18% are considering leaving the platform within the next year.

*“NOVA CART gives us orders, but sometimes maintaining the online catalogue takes more effort than the additional business is worth.”*

*“Customers blame us when the app says something is available but we don't actually have it.”*

# **7. Marketing**

NOVA CART currently spends approximately ₹17 lakh per month on promotions and customer acquisition. Around 58% of the marketing budget focuses on acquiring new customers, and management is considering increasing total marketing spending by another 30%.

· First-order discounts

· Referral rewards

· Social media advertising

· Influencer promotions

· Festival coupons

· Free-delivery campaigns

The marketing team believes more users will eventually create more repeat customers. The finance team disagrees.

# **8. Customer Support**

Support tickets increased from 3,100 to 5,900 per month.

· 29% — Refund status

· 24% — Delayed delivery

· 19% — Missing/unavailable products

· 13% — Coupon problems

· 9% — Incorrect orders

· 6% — Other

Average resolution time is 9.2 hours. Customer support currently relies on separate systems for orders, refunds and store communication.

# **9. Competitive Environment**

NOVA CART competes with large quick-commerce platforms, food-delivery applications expanding into groceries, supermarket delivery services and local stores accepting direct digital orders.

Larger competitors can often provide faster delivery and larger discounts. However, NOVA CART has a potential advantage: hundreds of independent local stores carrying products that may not be available through larger platforms. The company has not yet developed a clear strategy around this advantage.

# **10. Financial & Business Constraints**

**Maximum additional implementation budget for the next six months: ₹25 LAKH**

Management does not want solutions requiring:

· Massive physical infrastructure expansion

· Hundreds of new employees

· Opening warehouses across the cities

· Unsustainable customer discounts

The company is willing to change its application, internal workflows, store-facing systems, customer experience, pricing mechanisms, marketing strategy, data usage, delivery processes and partner-store experience.

· Customer mobile application

· Order database

· Customer database

# **12. Management Disagreement**

**CEO:** “We need to improve customer retention.”

**Marketing Head:** “We need stronger acquisition and better promotions.”

**Operations Head:** “Delivery reliability is damaging the experience.”

**Partner Manager:** “Inventory accuracy and store experience are the real bottlenecks.”

**Product Head:** “We're treating ourselves like another delivery app instead of giving customers a reason to choose local commerce.”

**Finance Head:** “Our problem isn't growth. It's the quality and cost of that growth.”

**Each position has supporting evidence. You decide what deserves priority.**

# **13. Your Challenge**

Act as NOVA CART's AI-powered Business Innovation Team. Use AI, Prompt Engineering, Business Reasoning, Research, Data and Creativity to investigate the business, identify a high-impact underlying problem or opportunity, and validate why it deserves attention.

**DESIGN AND DEVELOP A FUNCTIONAL WEB OR MOBILE APPLICATION THAT ADDRESSES IT.**

There is no predefined application to build and no single expected solution. Your investigation determines your product.

# **14. How to Approach the Challenge**

**DISCOVER —** Analyse the business information. Look beyond surface-level symptoms and identify patterns, relationships, contradictions and opportunities.

**PROMPT —** Use AI as a reasoning partner to investigate assumptions, generate hypotheses, compare possibilities, challenge conclusions and improve product decisions.

**VALIDATE —** Support your chosen problem using the case evidence and, where useful, external research and data.

**DESIGN —** Determine your target user, the problem being solved, why it matters, the required product flow and the features that create meaningful value.

**BUILD —** Develop a functional Web Application or Mobile Application demonstrating the core solution.

**PROVE —** Demonstrate how the solution can create measurable business value.

# **15. Research & Data Freedom**

Participants may strengthen their analysis and solution using:

· Web research

· Publicly accessible web scraping

· External/public datasets

· Kaggle and other open datasets

· Government/open-data sources

External information should have a clear purpose: validate an assumption, discover an opportunity, improve the product or support a decision.

# **16. Functional Prototype Requirement**

The application must demonstrate meaningful core functionality. Static UI screens alone are not considered a complete functional prototype.

**INPUT → PROCESSING / LOGIC → ACTION / RECOMMENDATION / OUTPUT**

Your demo should make it clear who uses the product, what they do, what the system does, and what business value is created.

· AI tools and AI-assisted coding

· No-code / low-code platforms

· Frontend and backend frameworks

· Databases

· Cloud and deployment platforms

· Public/free APIs

· Web scraping

· External datasets

· Open-source libraries

· AI-generated code

Paid API keys, LLM integration, custom AI/ML model training and custom AI infrastructure are not mandatory. Using more technology does not automatically make a solution stronger. Technology should serve the problem.

# **18. What You Must Submit**

**1. Problem Diagnosis —** Clearly explain the underlying problem or opportunity identified and the evidence supporting it.

**2. Prompt Journey —** Show the key prompts that influenced your analysis, reasoning and product decisions. Do not submit every AI conversation.

**3. Solution Explanation —** Explain what you built, who it is for and why this solution addresses the diagnosed problem.

**4. Functional Prototype —** Submit a working Web or Mobile Application demonstrating the core functionality.

**5. Source Code —** Provide the project repository containing your implementation.

**6. Demo —** Demonstrate the application's primary user journey and functionality.

**7. Business Impact —** Explain the measurable value your solution could create, such as revenue, retention, conversion, cost, time, customer satisfaction, productivity, accuracy or operational efficiency.
